Same 12 proposals, two ways to screen them

The usual request: qualify incoming applications before a person sees them. The naive way is to send every document to a model. It is expensive, and the client cannot see why someone was dropped. The other way is a cheap mechanical layer first, then the model only on who survived — with a quote from the text under every decision.
